## 1\. Project Name and Description

**Project Name:** Web-Based Gym Management and Promotional Website for Vinathletics

The project aims to develop a web-based platform that centralizes essential gym management activities and provides a public promotional website for Vinathletics. The management component will support member and membership records, walk-in transactions, cash and GCash payments, trainer availability, coaching schedules, and management reports. The promotional component will present gym services, facilities, fitness programs, announcements, events, and special offers to current and prospective customers.

The system will provide access appropriate to each user role, while allowing the general public to browse promotional information without signing in. It is intended to improve the organization and accessibility of gym-related information and support daily operations and management decision-making.

## 2\. Problem Statement

Vinathletics currently manages operations using manual records, including paper documents, notebooks, and spreadsheets. This approach presents difficulties in maintaining member and membership information, recording and tracking payments, coordinating trainer availability and coaching schedules, and preparing management reports. Limited online promotion also makes it harder to present gym services, facilities, events, and offers to prospective customers through a centralized platform.

The proposed system seeks to address these operational difficulties by consolidating relevant records and workflows into a web-based application and establishing a public-facing promotional website.

## 3\. General and Specific Objectives

### 3.1 General Objective

To develop a web-based gym management and promotional website for Vinathletics that supports the administration of gym operations, provides members with access to relevant services, and promotes the gym to the public, with an approximate development target of one month and no fixed completion date yet established.

### 3.2 Specific Objectives

1. To provide authorized administrators with capabilities to manage member information, membership plans and status, gym services, trainers, schedules, and promotional content.  
2. To support member self-registration, staff- and administrator-assisted registration, and online requests for membership renewal or plan changes; the approval procedures for these requests will be defined during requirements analysis.  
3. To support the recording of cash payments and automated GCash payment gateway transactions for applicable membership, walk-in, and coaching services, while maintaining accessible payment records.  
4. To allow staff to process non-member walk-in gym access without requiring membership registration.  
5. To allow members to view available coaching sessions, book sessions, and request or perform supported rescheduling and cancellation actions.  
6. To enable trainers to review assigned sessions and weekly schedules and update their availability, while administrators manage trainer assignments.  
7. To allow authorized management users to view and export reports concerning members, memberships, payments, revenue, trainers, and schedules.  
8. To publish gym announcements, special offers, events, facilities and equipment information, fitness programs, and services through a publicly accessible promotional website.  
9. To provide role-appropriate access to operational functions for Super Admin, Administrator, Staff, Trainer, and Member users.

## 5\. Project Scope

### 5.1 In Scope

1. **Member and Membership Management:** Member self-registration and authorized assisted registration; member profiles; time-based membership plans and status; online renewal and plan-change requests, with approval procedures to be defined during requirements analysis.  
2. **Walk-in Gym Services:** Staff-recorded per-session transactions for customers who do not need to register as members.  
3. **Payment Management:** Recording of cash payments by authorized counter personnel; automated GCash payment gateway integration; payment records and history for membership fees, renewals, walk-in access, and personal coaching.  
4. **Trainer and Schedule Management:** Trainer information, assigned sessions, weekly schedules, availability updates, and administrator-managed assignments.  
5. **Coaching Session Management:** Online viewing and booking of available personal coaching sessions, including supported rescheduling and cancellation.  
6. **Promotional Website Management:** Publication of announcements, gym news, special offers, events, facilities and equipment information, fitness programs, and services; public access to promotional pages.  
7. **Reporting:** Viewing and exporting member and membership, payment and revenue, and trainer and schedule reports.  
8. **User and Access Management:** Authenticated operational access with role-appropriate capabilities for Super Admin, Administrator, Staff, Trainer, and Member users.

### 5.2 Out of Scope

1. A native mobile application.  
2. Wearable-device integration.  
3. Advanced health monitoring and fitness-tracking functionality.  
4. Promotional activity reporting.  
5. Payment methods other than cash and GCash in the currently agreed initial scope.

## 7\. Project Constraints and Assumptions

### 7.1 Constraints

1. **Web-only delivery:** The initial system will operate through a web browser and will not include a native mobile application.  
2. **Internet dependency:** Access to the system requires an internet connection.  
3. **Development timeframe:** The team has been informed of an approximate one-month development period; no fixed completion date has been established. This replaces the original proposal's four-month target.  
4. **Payment scope:** Supported methods are limited to cash and GCash in the initial agreed scope. Automated GCash gateway integration is included, replacing the original proposal's exclusion of online gateway integration.  
5. **Functional exclusions:** Wearable integration, advanced health monitoring, and promotional activity reporting are not part of the initial scope.

### 7.2 Assumptions and Dependencies Requiring Validation

1. A suitable payment provider or integration arrangement will be available for automated GCash payments. Provider eligibility, onboarding, testing access, transaction handling, and related constraints must be validated before implementation commitments are finalized.  
2. Vinathletics representatives will be available to clarify operational policies and evaluate relevant workflows.  
3. The academic adviser and gym representative will participate in reviewing and approving applicable project requirements.  
4. Detailed business rules—including membership pricing and durations, approval of membership changes, booking restrictions, cancellation rules, payment exceptions, and report formats—will be defined in subsequent requirements documentation.  
5. The exact project deadline and milestones will be confirmed when the academic schedule or project management plan is finalized.
