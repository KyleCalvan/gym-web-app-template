# VinAthletics — User Stories in Implementation Order

Reviewed: October 26, 2026. Basis: the frontend source in this working tree, including navigation, rendered screens, forms, buttons, and their event handlers.

This inventory of abilities represented by the existing frontend is organized into a suggested chronological implementation sequence. The phases explain what to accomplish first and what depends on it; they are not a detailed implementation specification, a backend completion report, or an expansion of the project scope. The project proposal, backend models, and planned authentication changes are not evidence for adding abilities here. Runtime behavior was not browser-tested for this review.

**Customer means Member in the signed-in frontend.** A public visitor can browse without an account. A walk-in customer is an option in the staff POS; there is no separate walk-in dashboard. Account, member record, and purchased membership are distinct concepts.

**Access limitation:** [App.tsx](../src/App.tsx) sends real authenticated users to [AccountStatus.tsx](../src/auth/AccountStatus.tsx), which explicitly says personal records and operations are unavailable while integration is being completed. The full role dashboards below are wired into the development/demo flow. Their presence does not prove production access or backend authorization. Super Admin is a separate interface; this inventory does not assume it inherits every Admin screen.

Status terms used below:

- **UI:** A visible screen/control has frontend behavior. Most dashboard changes use in-memory state; this label does not mean a persistent backend feature is complete.
- **API-linked UI:** The frontend calls a service for the action. Service correctness was not assessed.
- **Demo:** Explicitly a development-only or simulated flow.
- **Partial:** The control exists, but its handler does not complete the outcome described by its label.

## How to follow this order

Work through the phases below in order, following the steps within each phase. The ordering is inferred only from dependencies between these user stories; it does not use GitHub issues or add new stories. Independent work can proceed once its stated prerequisites are available.

- **Keep the existing IDs:** PUB, CUS, TRN, STF, ADM, SUP, and ACC still identify the original actor or shared interface. Rows are regrouped across roles so setup comes before the actions that consume it.
- **Use the checkpoints:** Each phase states an observable outcome to verify. These are completion targets, not claims that the current frontend already meets them.
- **Preserve the evidence:** Every original story and its evidence/limitations is retained. UI, API-linked UI, Demo, and Partial describe the reviewed frontend, not implementation progress.
- **Resolve incomplete outcomes:** A Partial story or contradictory promise needs its intended behavior settled before being called complete. Missing approval pages, real payment processing, delivery services, and other unestablished abilities are not automatically added by this order.
- **Apply shared stories where available:** Account-status stories concern the real account screen; dashboard controls concern role interfaces currently in the demo flow. Public browsing requires no sign-in. Super Admin user management covers Admin, Member, Trainer, and Staff records, not other Super Admin accounts.

## Phase 1. Account access and shared navigation

**Prerequisites:** Start here. These controls provide the entry and role context used by the later phases.

**Order of work:**

1. Connect the public registration/login entry to Google sign-in, account status, required profile details, terms acceptance, and sign-out.
2. Use displayed Assigned Roles for account-context selection and, where authorized interfaces are supplied, role switching. Establish shared page navigation/search and appearance controls before connecting business screens.
3. Resolve the intended behavior of the incomplete password and two-step controls before claiming security completion. Their demo behavior is not evidence of production security.
4. Keep demo-role sign-in and local registration as development-only ways to exercise later stories; they are not prerequisites for real account registration.

**Completion checkpoint:** The supported account entry, completion, role-selection, navigation, and sign-out paths can be followed. Real accounts remaining on AccountStatus is still an integration gap; demo success does not establish operational access.

## Phase 2. Membership catalog and public gym information

**Prerequisites:** Phase 1 for admin access. Public informational content can be handled independently.

**Order of work:**

1. Create/edit plans and control their active status and active-plan limit.
2. Present the gym introduction, contact details, and advertised plans using the existing public screens.
3. Connect member plan comparison to the catalog before using plans in registration, promotions, or checkout. Current-plan comparisons become verifiable after member records are available in phase 3.

**Completion checkpoint:** Available plans can be maintained and compared consistently. Choosing a public plan opens login; it must not be mistaken for a purchase.

## Phase 4. Promotion setup and discovery

**Prerequisites:** Phase 2 for applicable plans and phase 3 for member-specific eligibility.

**Order of work:**

1. Create draft promotions, then support inspection, editing, publication, unpublication, filtering, and deletion.
2. Show published offers publicly and applicable offers in the member list and featured login modal.

**Completion checkpoint:** A prepared offer can be published and found in the appropriate existing screens, with its code and conditions visible. Public visibility alone does not establish member eligibility.

## Phase 7. Trainer availability and the coaching lifecycle

**Prerequisites:** Phase 3 for trainers/members, phase 5 for checkout, and phase 6 for applicable membership restrictions.

**Order of work:**

1. Maintain trainer availability blocks, then connect trainer discovery and the existing trainer/admin/staff schedule views. Resolve the documented static staff schedule and disconnected availability data.
2. Connect admin assignment and member booking checkout, followed by member booking lists and trainer session lists. Verify the correct trainer/member context and booking status.
3. Handle date/time edits, trainer change requests, and member/trainer cancellations through the existing controls. Resolve missing request handling and refund promises before calling those outcomes complete.
4. Complete the lifecycle with trainer session completion and member ratings; only completed, unrated sessions should reach the described rating action.

**Completion checkpoint:** Availability can be reviewed, a session arranged/booked, its status followed, and a completed session rated. Trainer isolation, conflicting times, Pending versus Confirmed wording, and the absent change-request approval flow remain explicit checks.

## Interpretation limits and abilities not established by this frontend

These findings prevent visible controls or wording from being mistaken for completed features. They are observations, not additional user stories or proposed scope.

1. **Dashboard integration:** Real authenticated accounts remain on the account-status interface. Demo dashboards and real account completion must not be presented as one completed operational flow.
2. **Payments:** Member checkout, staff POS, and admin-assisted renewal use the shared payment simulator. It allows selecting success/failure and retrying a failed test. No actual GCash charge, cash settlement, automatic refund, or payment-confirmation email is established by these handlers. Member/staff histories deliberately hide simulated transactions.
3. **Membership cancellation:** Copy about continued access, prorated refunds, and admin confirmation differs from the immediate local state change. No cancellation-approval queue was found. Frozen-state messaging also does not prove every plan-change path enforces that state.
4. **Student review and schedule requests:** Submission controls exist, but student verification is local Pending state and trainer schedule-change submission is an audit entry/toast. There are no connected accept/reject screens for these requests.
5. **Scheduling data:** Member booking checks compare against existing sessions, but checkout is not connected to the trainer's availability blocks. Staff's weekly schedule is sample data. Admin session assignment also does not establish the same availability validation as member checkout.
6. **Trainer identity:** App passes the complete sessions collection to TrainerDashboard and TrainerSessions, which do not filter it by current trainer. TrainerSchedule does filter by current trainer. TrainerProfile expects a `trainer` prop that App's generic rendering does not supply. Stories describe the visible role intent, not verified isolation or working profile persistence.
7. **Reports and totals:** Admin report filters do not constrain chart data or exported rows. Several revenue/membership charts use fixed datasets; some “MTD” totals are not restricted to the current month. Staff “My Transactions” is date-scoped, not cashier-scoped. Do not infer accurate accounting or filtered reporting from titles alone.
8. **User administration and security:** Super Admin's role selector does not enact role reassignment. Password recovery/change controls and demo email-code verification do not implement production account security. The real account-status screen mentions authenticator requirements but offers no enrollment or challenge form. There is no visible create/manage-other-superadmins flow or configurable permission matrix.
9. **Session termination and backups:** Dashboard session termination only edits local state. Backup download is a frontend snapshot; upload and restore are mock operations. There is no scheduled-backup or database-restore management workflow established here.
10. **Communication:** Member/trainer navigation connects to Gym Links & QR. A leftover shared ChatView file is not evidence of a reachable messaging feature. Notification preferences and confirmation text do not prove email/SMS dispatch.
11. **Walk-ins:** Staff can choose a walk-in customer at POS, but the sale catalog is based on membership plans. A distinct per-visit walk-in product, price configuration, visit entitlement, or walk-in self-service portal is not established.
12. **Other absent management flows:** No connected page was found for general announcements/events publishing, facilities/equipment administration, workout/diet programming, inventory, payroll, student approval, or membership-request approval. Public marketing text and the project overview do not add those abilities to this inventory. The footer's Privacy/Terms links point to Contact; the actual Terms of Use reader is in account/registration flows.

The evidence above is limited to frontend source and reachable navigation/form wiring. It does not certify persistence, server permissions, external delivery, financial correctness, or end-to-end completion.
